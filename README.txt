BATTERY INVENTORY V4.4

FIX:
The Battery History "View full history" button now uses the actual purchase-batch ID,
rather than a composite battery-name key. This is more reliable on iPhone Safari.

The history view shows:
- Total bought
- Purchase date, quantity, buying price and supplier for every batch
- Total sold
- Sale date, quantity, selling price and vehicle model
- Sales revenue and gross profit

Also retained:
- Vehicle model search
- Monthly spending/cash-in calculations
- Automatic cash entries for new purchases and sales
- Backup/restore/clear
- V2/V3 migration
